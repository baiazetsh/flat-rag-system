💡 About This Visualization

  • PCA (Principal Component Analysis) reduces 1024-dimensional vectors to 2D space
  • Each point represents a text chunk from your documents
  • Points close together have similar semantic meaning
  • Clusters indicate topics or themes in your data
  • Use this to verify vector quality and distribution

⚙️ How to Use

  • Select Collection: Choose which vector collection to visualize
  • Adjust Vectors: More vectors = more detailed view (but slower)
  • Update Plot: Click to regenerate with new settings
  • Interpret: Look for clusters, outliers, and overall distribution

🎯 What to Look For

  • Good clustering: Points grouped by topic indicate quality embeddings
  • Even distribution: Shows diverse content coverage
  • Outliers: May indicate unique content or potential noise
  • Dense regions: Suggest common themes or repeated content
